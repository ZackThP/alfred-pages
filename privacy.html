Alfred privacy policy

Last updated 11 October 2026

Alfred is a personal assistant built and used by one person, its developer and owner. This policy explains what Google account data Alfred accesses, why, and what happens to it.

What Alfred accesses

With the account owner's consent, Alfred accesses:

  • Google Calendar to read events and to create events for deadlines.
  • Gmail to read and summarize university email, and to send or modify messages only when the owner asks.

How the data is used

The data is used only to provide Alfred's features to the account owner: planning the day, summarizing email and tracking deadlines. It is not used for advertising, profiling or any other purpose.

Where the data is stored

Access tokens and the data Alfred fetches are stored on the owner's own computer. The developer does not operate a server that collects this data.

Sharing and AI processing

Alfred does not sell or share Google user data. To produce its answers, Alfred sends the relevant text, such as an email summary or a list of events, to a third-party AI model provider, solely to generate a response for the account owner. Google user data is not used to train AI models. No person reads the data other than the account owner.

Google API Services User Data Policy

Alfred's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access and deleting data

The owner can revoke Alfred's access at any time in the Google Account settings under Security, then Third-party apps and services. Deleting Alfred's local token and data files on the owner's computer removes everything Alfred has stored.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date above will be updated.

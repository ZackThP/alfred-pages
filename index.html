Alfred

A personal assistant that helps one person keep track of their classes, deadlines and email.

What it does

Alfred reads the owner's university email and course deadlines each evening, adds major deadlines to the owner's own Google Calendar, and helps plan the day around classes and work. It runs on the owner's own computer.

Who it is for

Alfred is a private tool built and used by one person. It is not offered to the public and has no other users.

Google account access

Alfred asks for access to the owner's Google Calendar and Gmail only so it can do the tasks above. How that data is used and protected is described in the privacy policy.
